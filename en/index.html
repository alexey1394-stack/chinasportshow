    How it's calculated
    your price

    You'll see the same chain line by line in the quotation: where every figure comes from.

    01

    Factory invoice

    The manufacturer's list price in US dollars. Converted at the Central Bank rate plus a buffer, so currency moves during production don't hit you unexpectedly.

    02

    Container

    Freight from Qingdao and delivery from customs to your city. If your order doesn't fill a container, it goes in a shared one and you pay only for your cubic metres.

    03

    Customs

    Fee, duty, import VAT, broker. Everything is calculated from the customs value and shown as separate lines.

    04

    Our 15 %

    The commission is charged on the delivered cost — the same for the whole project, no different markups per item.

    That's why the planner prices below aren't guesswork: they're calculated with the same chain from the factory's current price list and our latest factory invoices. The total stays approximate until your city and the exact set are known.
