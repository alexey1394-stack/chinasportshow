1. The Operator

The personal data operator is CIAPO Limited Liability Company (OOO «СИАПО») (INN 6900029331, OGRN 1266900002380), hereinafter the “Operator”. Website: chinasportshow.ru. Contact for personal data matters: phone and MAX +7 (915) 771-69-81, Telegram @china_sport_show.

This Policy has been drawn up in accordance with Federal Law of the Russian Federation No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 “On Personal Data” and sets out how the personal data of website visitors are processed and protected.

2. What data and why

The Operator processes only the data that the visitor enters in the request form:

  • name, phone number, city, preferred contact method, comment — to respond to the request, prepare a commercial quotation and contact the visitor in the way they prefer;
  • parameters of the gym configured in the planner (room dimensions, selected zones, equipment list, estimated cost) — so that the quotation matches the request. This information is not personal data, but it is sent together with the request.

The purpose of processing is to review the request, prepare and send a quotation, and conclude and perform an equipment supply contract. The Operator has no other purposes, including mailings.

The legal basis is the consent of the data subject (Article 6(1)(1) of Law 152-FZ), which the visitor gives by a separate tick in the form before sending the request, and the necessity of processing to conclude a contract at the data subject's initiative (Article 6(1)(5) of Law 152-FZ).

3. What the website does not collect

  • the website does not use cookies, visitor counters, pixels or analytics systems;
  • the website does not request data automatically: until the form is sent, no information about the visitor is transferred to the Operator;
  • special categories of data (health, beliefs, etc.) and biometric data are not processed.

The gym planner and the cost estimate run in the visitor's browser. To display exchange rates, the page requests open data of the Bank of Russia (the cbr-xml-daily.ru service); no personal data are transferred in the process.

4. How data are processed and where they are stored

A request from the form goes to a secure receiver, from which it is forwarded within one minute to the Operator's responsible employee and recorded in the Operator's client database located in the territory of the Russian Federation. Processing is carried out both with and without automation tools (when communicating with the visitor).

Processing period: until the purpose is achieved — a response to the request and, if a contract is concluded, until it is fully performed and the document retention periods established by law have expired. Requests that did not result in a contract are destroyed or anonymised no later than 1 year after receipt.

The Operator does not transfer personal data to third parties, except as expressly required by law, and does not entrust their processing to other persons without notifying the data subject.

5. Protection measures

  • only authorised employees of the Operator have access to requests;
  • the request receiver accepts only new records from the website; reading records from the website is impossible;
  • data are transmitted over a secure connection (HTTPS);
  • the forwarding of each request to the responsible employee is logged.

6. Visitor's rights

The data subject has the right to:

  1. obtain information about the processing of their data;
  2. demand that the data be clarified, blocked or destroyed if they are incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or processed in breach of the law;
  3. withdraw consent to processing at any time;
  4. appeal against the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor (the Russian data protection authority) or to court.

To exercise these rights, simply message us on Telegram or MAX (contacts above) from the same phone number that was given in the request, or call the phone number above. The Operator responds within no more than 10 working days. If consent is withdrawn, processing stops and the data are destroyed within 30 days, unless otherwise required by law.

7. Changes to the Policy

The Operator may amend this Policy. The current version is always available at chinasportshow.ru/policy/ (Russian original) and chinasportshow.ru/en/policy/ (English translation). The version date is stated at the beginning of the document.
